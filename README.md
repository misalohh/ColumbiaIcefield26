Colombia Icefield 26 is a model based on my research and calculations that shows my predictions of the decrease in mass of the ice field in Alberta over the next 100 years. There are three settings partially based on the Representative Concentration Pathways (RCP) climate scenarios set out by the Government of Canada GoC-ClimateScenarios which are represented as low, medium and high levels of warming; medium being the most likely prediction of global temperature increase. After choosing a setting, you can scroll through the next 100 years and watch as the ice ball shrinks showing a visual representation of the prediction.
